Welcome to Muddle Puddle Café

Welcome to Muddle Puddle Café, your local café serving freshly brewed coffee, all-day breakfast, homemade waffles, Eggs Benedict, burgers, and authentic Nepalese favourites including Momos and Chowmein. Whether you're catching up with friends or grabbing breakfast on the go, we'd love to see you. Experience the perfect blend of local flavors and international cuisine!
